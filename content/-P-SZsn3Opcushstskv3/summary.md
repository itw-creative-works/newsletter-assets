Open models are becoming more useful because you can study how they work, adapt them, and keep using them even as newer versions appear. At the same time, the newest frontier models are improving fast, especially for coding and agent-like tasks, which makes knowing how to choose and use them more important than ever. For students, the big takeaway is simple: the strongest AI habit is not chasing the flashiest tool, but learning how to ask better questions and guide the model you have.
