Smaller agencies are learning that hitting a spend threshold does not always mean cleaner access or lower fees in programmatic buying. That matters because every extra layer cuts into margin, which is a useful reminder for anyone measuring offline-to-online campaigns: the economics behind the channel shape how far your budget actually goes. In this issue, we also look at how the same discipline used in link building and automation can help teams pick the right pages, prioritize the right work, and build repeatable workflows.
